<B>Scenario</B>
An elderly person or someone with limited mobility often forgets to switch off
the room lights during the day and finds it difficult to locate or reach the switch in the
dark at night.

<B>Problem Statement</B>
 Design an Arduino-based system that automatically turns lights
 ON during the night and OFF during the day using a light-dependent sensor.

<B>Components:</B>
* Arduino Uno
* LDR (Light Dependent Resistor) Sensor
* LED or Bulb (as Load)
* Jumper Wires, resistor, and Breadboard

<B>How It Works:</B>
The LDR sensor continuously monitors the ambient light level for a set threshold. For its
lower values (indicating night), the Arduino switches ON the light. Whereas, for its
higher values (during the day), the Arduino turns the light OFF to conserve energy. This
automatic operation ensures comfort and safety, particularly for elderly individuals or
those with limited mobility who may find it difficult to operate switches manually.
