<B>Scenario</B>
Classrooms become hot and fans aren't always turned on promptly.

<B>Problem Statement</B>
Design a system that automatically controls a fan based on room temperature.

<B>Components:</B>
    * Arduino Uno
    * DHT11 Temperature & Humidity Sensor
    * DC Motor to run a Fan
    * Jumper Wires

<B>How It Works:</B>
The DHT11 sensor continuously monitors the temperature. If it crosses a preset threshold (e.g., 30°C), the Arduino turns on the fan. When it drops below the threshold, the fan is turned off.
