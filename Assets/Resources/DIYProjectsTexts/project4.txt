<B>Scenario</B>
Touching door handles can spread germs and is inconvenient when carrying items.
Problem: Build a small door that opens automatically when someone approaches, with an LED to show when it’s opening.

<B>Components:</B>
    • Arduino Uno
    • Ultrasonic Sensor
    • Servo Motor
    • LED (any color)
    • Resistor (220 Ω for LED)
    • Jumper Wires, Breadboard

<B>How It Works:</B>
    • The ultrasonic sensor keeps checking the distance.
    • If a person is within about 30 cm, the LED turns on and the servo rotates to open the door.
    • After a short delay, the servo returns to the closed position and the LED turns off.
