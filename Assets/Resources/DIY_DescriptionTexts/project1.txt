<B>Scenario</B>
Lights are often left on in empty rooms, wasting electricity.

<B>Problem Statement</B>
Create a system that turns the lights on when motion is detected and off when no one is present.

<B>Components:</B>
    * Arduino Uno
    * PIR Motion Sensor
    * LED/Bulb
    * Jumper Wires, Breadboard

<B>How It Works:</B>
The PIR sensor detects motion in the room. When movement is detected, the Arduino turns on the light. After a few seconds of no motion, it automatically turns the light off.
